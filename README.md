### Hi, I'm Rubaiya 👋

I'm an ML researcher in the making, currently based in Cologne and applying for PhDs in Germany. I finished my Master's at TH Köln in 2025, where my thesis pulled me deep into generative models and how to evaluate them honestly.

I care about empirical rigor and trustworthy AI. The kind of papers I find most interesting are the ones that push back on overclaimed results and ask whether models actually understand what we think they understand. That's the direction I want my research to go, especially in the space of large language models, reasoning models,
and AI safety.

Outside of research, I came to ML through engineering. Before my thesis I worked with Kubernetes, Terraform, and cloud infrastructure at Detecon (Deutsche Telekom). I think that engineering background helps me think about ML systems end to end, not just the model.

I'm at the start of my research career and learning fast. If my work overlaps with yours, I'd love to hear from you.
